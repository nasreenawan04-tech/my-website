
# Comprehensive Prompt for 30-Minute Animated Cartoon Story Script Generation

## Main Prompt

Create a compelling 30-minute animated cartoon story script about [YOUR STORY TOPIC] in the following format:

## REQUIREMENTS:
- **Duration**: 30 minutes (approximately 9,000-9,500 words total)
- **Tone**: [Adventure/Comedy/Drama/Fantasy/Action] - engaging and age-appropriate
- **Structure**: Divided into 5 acts (approximately 6 minutes each)
- **Format**: Ready for voiceover with integrated visual prompts for animation
- **Target Audience**: [Children 6-12/Teens 13-17/Family/Young Adults]

## SCRIPT FORMAT:

For each scene, provide:

1. **SCENE DESCRIPTION** with numbered reference (e.g., SCENE 1, SCENE 2)
   - Location and time of day
   - Characters present
   - Mood and atmosphere

2. **Midjourney Prompt** in code block format with these specifications:
   - Detailed character and environment description
   - Cartoon/animation art style (Disney, Pixar, anime, comic book, etc.)
   - Color palette and lighting
   - Emotional tone
   - Technical parameters (--ar 16:9 --v 6 --style [cartoon style])

3. **Runway Gen-3 Animation Prompt** in code block format with:
   - Character movements and expressions
   - Camera angles (close-up, wide shot, over-shoulder, pan, zoom)
   - Action sequences (running, jumping, flying, transformations)
   - Background motion (wind, water, crowd movement)
   - Duration (3-10 seconds recommended)
   - Animation style notes (smooth, exaggerated, bouncy, fluid)

4. **DIALOGUE & NARRATION**:
   - Character names in CAPS before dialogue
   - Natural, age-appropriate conversation
   - Include [ACTION], [EMOTION], [SOUND EFFECT] markers
   - Narrator text for scene transitions
   - Include [PAUSE] for comedic or dramatic timing
   - Sound effects written as [SFX: description]

## STRUCTURE - 5 ACTS:

### ACT 1: SETUP & INTRODUCTION (0:00 - 6:00)

**Opening Hook (0:00-1:00)**: 
- Exciting or intriguing opening scene
- Introduce main character in action or conflict
- Establish the story world

**World Building (1:00-3:00)**:
- Show the normal world/daily life
- Introduce supporting characters
- Establish relationships and dynamics
- Hint at character's wants/needs

**Inciting Incident (3:00-6:00)**:
- Problem or opportunity presents itself
- Main character's world is disrupted
- Stakes are established
- Act ends with character making a decision

### ACT 2: RISING ACTION (6:00 - 12:00)

**New Challenges (6:00-8:00)**:
- Character embarks on journey/quest
- First obstacles appear
- New allies or mentors introduced
- Learning and growth begins

**Complications (8:00-10:00)**:
- Obstacles become more difficult
- Character faces failures or setbacks
- Relationships tested
- Tension builds

**False Victory or Defeat (10:00-12:00)**:
- Things seem to go well OR everything falls apart
- Character thinks they've succeeded/failed
- Cliffhanger: new information changes everything
- Act ends with major revelation

### ACT 3: MIDPOINT & ESCALATION (12:00 - 18:00)

**Game Changer (12:00-14:00)**:
- Major plot twist or revelation
- Character's approach must change
- New understanding of the stakes
- Villain/antagonist revealed or empowered

**Preparation & Gathering (14:00-16:00)**:
- Character regroups with allies
- New plan is formed
- Montage of preparation/training
- Character growth moment

**Point of No Return (16:00-18:00)**:
- Character commits fully to goal
- Relationships reach breaking or bonding point
- Final obstacle before climax revealed
- Act ends with everything on the line

### ACT 4: CLIMAX (18:00 - 24:00)

**The Big Confrontation (18:00-20:00)**:
- Main character faces biggest challenge
- All skills/lessons are put to use
- Spectacular action sequence
- Allies contribute to the fight

**Darkest Moment (20:00-22:00)**:
- All seems lost
- Character must make ultimate sacrifice or choice
- Emotional low point
- Internal character conflict resolved

**Victory & Transformation (22:00-24:00)**:
- Character overcomes through growth/change
- Clever solution or powerful moment
- Villain defeated or problem solved
- Act ends with victory but loose ends remain

### ACT 5: RESOLUTION & EPILOGUE (24:00 - 30:00)

**Aftermath (24:00-26:00)**:
- Immediate consequences of climax
- Character reunites with loved ones
- Rewards or recognition
- World returns to (new) normal

**Character Growth Showcase (26:00-28:00)**:
- Show how character has changed
- Relationships have evolved
- Lessons learned are clear
- Tie up remaining plot threads

**Final Message & Future Hook (28:00-30:00)**:
- Moral or theme reinforced
- Satisfying emotional conclusion
- Optional: sequel tease or future adventure hint
- Heartwarming or triumphant final moment

## VISUAL REQUIREMENTS:

- **Minimum 60-80 scenes total**
- Mix of action sequences, dialogue scenes, and quiet moments
- Varied camera angles and shot types
- Character close-ups for emotional moments
- Wide establishing shots for new locations
- Dynamic action sequences with movement
- Reaction shots and comedic timing beats
- Montage sequences for training/passage of time

## MIDJOURNEY PROMPT STYLE GUIDE:

Always include these elements:
- **Art Style**: Specific animation style (Pixar 3D, Disney 2D, anime, comic book, etc.)
- **Character Details**: Appearance, expressions, costumes
- **Environment**: Detailed setting description
- **Color Palette**: Vibrant/muted/pastel/bold colors
- **Lighting**: Time of day, mood lighting, magical glow, etc.
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, dynamic angles
- **Technical**: --ar 16:9 --v 6 --style [specific style]
- **Quality**: Professional animation concept art, high detail

Example template:
```
[Character description] in [action/pose], [environment details], [art style] animation style, [color palette], [lighting conditions], [emotional mood], professional character design, expressive features, dynamic composition, vibrant colors, high detail --ar 16:9 --v 6 --style [cartoon/anime/3d]
```

## RUNWAY GEN-3 ANIMATION PROMPT STYLE GUIDE:

Always specify:
- **Character Action**: Specific movements, gestures, expressions
- **Camera Movement**: Pan, zoom, orbit, track, static, handheld feel
- **Speed**: Slow-motion, normal speed, fast-paced action
- **Animation Quality**: Smooth, fluid, exaggerated, bouncy
- **Effects**: Particle effects, speed lines, impact frames
- **Duration**: 3-10 seconds (be specific)
- **Style Notes**: Cartoon physics, realistic motion, anime-style action

Example template:
```
Camera: [Movement and angle]
Action: [Character movements and expressions]
Environment: [Background elements in motion]
Effects: [Special effects, particles, impacts]
Duration: [X] seconds
Style: [Animation quality and feel]
```

## CHARACTER DIALOGUE GUIDELINES:

- **Age-Appropriate Language**: Match vocabulary to target audience
- **Distinct Voices**: Each character has unique speech patterns
- **Natural Flow**: Include interruptions, pauses, reactions
- **Emotional Beats**: Show feelings through dialogue
- **Humor**: Age-appropriate jokes and wordplay
- **Pacing**: Vary sentence length for rhythm
- **Subtext**: What characters don't say is important too
- **Action During Dialogue**: Characters do things while talking

Format:
```
CHARACTER NAME: [Emotional direction] Dialogue here. [Action description]

NARRATOR: [If needed] Scene-setting or transition text.

[SFX: Sound effect description]
```

## CONTENT GUIDELINES:

### Story Elements:
- **Clear Protagonist**: Relatable main character with wants and needs
- **Compelling Goal**: Something specific to achieve
- **Worthy Obstacles**: Challenges that test the character
- **Character Arc**: Clear growth from beginning to end
- **Theme**: Universal message about friendship, courage, family, etc.
- **Stakes**: What happens if character fails?
- **Supporting Cast**: Colorful allies and memorable antagonists

### Emotional Beats:
- Moments of humor (every 2-3 minutes)
- Heartfelt emotional scenes
- Exciting action sequences
- Quiet character moments
- Surprising twists
- Satisfying payoffs to setups

### Pacing:
- Start with action or intrigue
- Balance quiet and loud moments
- Build tension progressively
- Release tension with humor or resolution
- Each act ends with hook for next act
- Final act delivers emotional satisfaction

## SPECIFIC SCENES TO INCLUDE:

1. **Opening Action Sequence** - Hook the audience
2. **Character Introduction** - Show personality through action
3. **World Establishment** - What's normal for this character?
4. **Call to Adventure** - The inciting incident
5. **Refusal/Acceptance** - Character's initial reaction
6. **Meeting the Mentor/Ally** - Support system introduced
7. **First Major Challenge** - Learning experience
8. **Moment of Doubt** - Character questions themselves
9. **Training/Preparation Montage** - Growth shown visually
10. **Relationship Building** - Bonding with allies
11. **Mid-Point Twist** - Game-changing revelation
12. **All Is Lost Moment** - Darkest before dawn
13. **Character's Choice** - Defining moment showing growth
14. **Climactic Battle/Confrontation** - All skills tested
15. **Victory Through Growth** - Win because of character change
16. **Emotional Resolution** - Relationships affirmed
17. **New Status Quo** - Changed world, changed character
18. **Final Triumph** - Satisfying conclusion

## ANIMATION-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS:

### Action Sequences:
- Describe key poses and in-betweens
- Impact frames for powerful moments
- Speed lines and motion blur effects
- Exaggerated movements for emphasis
- Camera shake for intensity
- Slow-motion for dramatic moments

### Facial Expressions:
- Extreme expressions for comedy
- Subtle expressions for drama
- Reaction shots
- Expression changes mid-dialogue
- Eyes tell the story

### Background Action:
- Supporting characters reacting
- Environmental storytelling
- Sight gags in background
- Continuous world feeling alive
- Foreground, mid-ground, background layers

## GENRE-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS:

### For Adventure Stories:
- Exotic locations
- Physical challenges
- Discovery moments
- Chase sequences
- Puzzle-solving

### For Comedy Stories:
- Visual gags every 30-60 seconds
- Callback jokes
- Physical comedy
- Wordplay and puns
- Character-based humor

### For Fantasy Stories:
- Magic system rules
- Fantastical creatures
- Transformation sequences
- Magical battles
- Enchanted locations

### For Action Stories:
- Hand-to-hand combat
- Vehicle chases
- Explosions and destruction
- Strategic planning
- Power-ups or special abilities
